Heisenberg Ring — Spin Dynamics

Every site carries a spin vector ⟨Sⱼ⟩ = (⟨Xⱼ⟩, ⟨Yⱼ⟩, ⟨Zⱼ⟩). Press play and watch the Néel pattern melt, precess, and partly re-form as the ring evolves under exact dynamics.

Arrows are the local spins, coloured by ⟨Zⱼ⟩ (up = warm, down = cool). Arrow length is |⟨Sⱼ⟩| ≤ 1 — it shrinks when a site grows entangled with the rest of the ring. The dashed arc is the periodic bond q5→q0.

Total magnetization Mz(t)

Mz = Σⱼ⟨Zⱼ⟩. It stays flat only when Jx = Jy and hx = 0; otherwise the anisotropy and field break the conservation.

Initial state |ψ₀⟩ = |010101⟩ (qubit-0-first) is a Néel state: alternating up/down along z. Local spins are exact expectation values of the diagonalized N=6 Hamiltonian, recomputed in-browser on every slider change.
