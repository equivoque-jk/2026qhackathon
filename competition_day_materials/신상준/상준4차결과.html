Why even_odd_grouped is best: In even_odd_grouped, within each parity layer (even bonds, then odd bonds), all XX terms come first, then all YY, then all ZZ. The XX bonds in the even layer are (0,1), (2,3), (4,5) — acting on disjoint qubit pairs. This means that after XX(0,1), the next gate YY(0,1) shares the same qubit pair, so the boundary CX between RXX and RYY can cancel (the trailing CX of RXX is the leading CX of RYY on the same bond).

In even_odd (type-major), XX(0,1) is followed by XX(1,2) which shares qubit 1 — preventing the transpiler from commuting YY(0,1) next to XX(0,1) to cancel CX gates. Result: even_odd_grouped achieves 18 CX (opt=3) vs 36 CX for even_odd.

Problem 2(a) — Exact Autocorrelation Function

$$A(t) = \langle\psi_0|e^{-iHt}|\psi_0\rangle = \sum_n |\langle E_n|\psi_0\rangle|^2\, e^{-iE_n t}$$
Analysis:
  • Quasi-periodic behavior: A(t) is a superposition of complex exponentials $e^{-iE_n t}$ with different frequencies. The function is quasi-periodic (not strictly periodic) because the eigenvalues $E_n$ are generally incommensurate.
  • |A(t)| ≤ 1: By Cauchy-Schwarz, $|A(t)| \leq 1$. At t=0, A(0)=1. For t>0, $|A(t)|<1$ in general (unless $|\psi_0\rangle$ is an eigenstate).
  • Recurrences: |A(t)| occasionally returns close to 1 (quantum revivals) when phases realign partially.

Analysis:
  • Peaks at eigenenergies: The DFT peaks appear exactly at $E_n$ where $|\langle E_n|\psi_0\rangle|^2 > 0$. Eigenstates with zero overlap produce no peak.
  • Hanning window: Reduces spectral leakage, sharpening the peaks at true eigenenergies and suppressing artifacts from the finite time window.
  • Energy convention: The sign $E = -2\pi f$ comes from $A(t) = \sum_n |c_n|^2 e^{-iE_n t}$, so positive energies correspond to negative DFT frequencies.
  • Spectroscopy connection: This technique is used in quantum phase estimation and quantum spectroscopy — measuring A(t) on a quantum computer and Fourier-transforming gives the energy spectrum of H with resolution ~2π/T_max.
